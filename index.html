HOW IT WORKS

Methodology

Minimal input

Enter only the Company name and Quantity. Growfolio resolves the exchange symbol and retrieves available public market data automatically.

Market data

Growfolio resolves the company through a market search endpoint, then downloads the latest quote and approximately one year of daily history.

Beta & standard deviation

Beta is covariance of daily stock returns with NIFTY 50 returns divided by NIFTY 50 return variance. Standard deviation is daily return sample deviation annualized with √252.

Return, CAGR & drawdown

1Y Return measures price change across the downloaded history. CAGR annualizes that growth over the exact date span. Max drawdown is the worst peak-to-trough decline during the same period.
